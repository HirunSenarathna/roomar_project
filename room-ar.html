RoomAR controls

  1. Enter AR and move your phone slowly to scan the room.
  2. Furniture needs a horizontal floor surface; curtains need a vertical wall surface.
  3. When the green reticle appears, tap the screen to place the selected product.
  4. After placement, touch an object to select it. Drag with one finger to move it on its current surface.
  5. Pinch with two fingers to resize. Twist two fingers to rotate. Colour/delete remain available in the toolbar.
  6. For curtains, use wider/narrower, taller/shorter and open/close controls.
