RoomAR controls

  1. Enter AR and move your phone slowly to scan the room.
  2. Furniture needs a horizontal floor surface; curtains need a vertical wall surface.
  3. When the green reticle appears, tap the screen to place the selected product.
  4. After placement, touch an object to select it. Drag with one finger to move it on its current surface.
  5. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in or out. Twist two fingers to rotate the object left or right. Room AR objects remain upright on their detected surface.
  6. Use Hide UI beside Next when you need a clearer camera view. Tap Show UI to restore the controls.
  7. For curtains, use wider/narrower, taller/shorter and open/close controls.
