How to demonstrate RoomAR

Use a well-lit room with visible floor texture. For curtains, choose a wall with edges or visible texture rather than a blank glossy surface.

1

Deploy over HTTPS

Publish this folder with GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel. Camera and WebXR features require a secure context on a phone.

2

Test marker mode

Open Marker Preview, show the Hiro marker on another screen or print it, and point the camera at it. Switch among the available products.

3

Start room mode

Tap Enter AR and move the phone slowly. Keep the camera aimed at a textured floor until the placement ring becomes visible.

4

Place furniture

Select Sofa, Chair, Table or Plant. Tap while the green placement ring is visible. Use Move, Rotate, Size, Colour and Delete to edit it.

5

Check collision handling

Try placing or moving one furniture item into another. RoomAR rejects the overlap and shows a warning.

6

Add curtains

Select Curtains and point the phone at a vertical surface. Place the curtain when the vertical surface indicator is valid, then adjust width, height, colour and open/close state.

Browser support: If your device does not expose WebXR immersive AR with hit testing, the room mode will show a compatibility message. Use an AR-capable Android phone with current Chrome for the markerless demonstration.
